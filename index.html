    How is this calculated?

    We tokenize both texts, strip common stop-words, and extract the most frequent meaningful terms from the job description. The match score is the percentage of those terms that also appear in your resume. Readability uses the Flesch Reading Ease formula (sentence length + syllable count) to estimate how easy your resume is to skim.
